	3. Security Best Practices
* Principle of Least Privilege: Ensure the IAM role assigned to your ProtocolHub function is scoped strictly to the required resources, avoiding the use of wildcards (*) which can be blocked by your current security safeguards.
* Idempotency: Because your environment utilizes asynchronous retries, ensure your payload handler is idempotent to prevent duplicate execution triggers during sync failures.
* Dead-Letter Handling: Ensure that any payload exceeding the defined threshold is shunted to a Dead-Letter Queue (DLQ) rather than stalling the execution pipeline.
